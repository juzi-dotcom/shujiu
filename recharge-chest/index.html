②
③

Runs from launch until the last day of every month. Rewards are sent automatically at 7pm Saudi time (6pm Egypt time) on that day.

  1. Open to regular users only — resellers are not eligible to participate.
  2. Recharges made through Google Pay, Apple In-App Purchase and in-app third-party payment methods are counted directly toward the event total.
  3. Diamonds bought by a reseller for himself do not count. Diamonds bought from a reseller are counted at the platform price.
  4. Rewards are distributed automatically as soon as the eligibility conditions are met.
  5. Any form of cheating, abuse or fraudulent behaviour is strictly prohibited. Detected users are disqualified immediately and all rewards are revoked.

Event Rules ✕
  1. The event starts at 6:00 PM (Egypt time) and 7:00 PM (Saudi time) on the last day of the previous month, and ends at the same time on the last day of the current month.
  2. All rewards will be automatically distributed when the month ends.
  3. TOP 1 user will receive Dias equal to 7% of the total recharge; TOP 2 will receive Dias equal to 3% of the total recharge.

怎么用这份线框图

✎ 改文字

画布内带虚下划线的文字都能改：点一下直接输入。
鼠标悬停会变粉底，聚焦变蓝底。
顶部「✎ 文字可编辑」按钮可整体开关。

📐 尺寸标注

红色小标签是布局尺寸提示（对应 750 设计稿的 rem 值）。
顶部「📐 尺寸标注」可隐藏。

💬 设计批注

蓝色小卡片是设计意图与已知风险，包括"现版坑在哪、本版怎么修"。
顶部「✎ 设计批注」可隐藏。

🎨 调间距字号

所有间距 / 字号 / 灰阶 / 圆角集中在文件顶部 :root 的 CSS 变量里，改一处全局生效。

🖨 打印 / 转 PDF

直接 Ctrl+P（Mac: Cmd+P），已配好打印样式：工具栏隐藏、文字转深色。

灰阶只表达层级，不代表最终配色。
